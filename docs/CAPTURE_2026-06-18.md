## Gems = damage + stats (the model)

`UpdatePattern.pattern` (the Common Weapon Gem, Lvl 5):
```json
{"ID":1,"Quality":5,"Level":5,"STR":6,"END":2,"DEX":4,"INT":6,"WIS":4,"LUK":2,
 "Wild":0.1,"EquipSpot":2,"Base":31,"Name":"Common Weapon","Power":24}
```
- **Weapon damage range = `Base·(1−Wild) .. Base·(1+Wild)`** → 31·0.9..31·1.1 = **27..34** (matches the tooltip "27–34 DAMAGE"). This is the real `DmgMin/DmgMax`.
- `Quality` = rarity tier (5=Common; the Uncommon helm gem was higher). `Level` scales Base + stats. `Power` is the tooltip power rating.
- A weapon gem's `EquipSpot:2` (Weapon). A **helm gem gives flat HEALTH** ("17 HEALTH") instead of a damage Base; an un-gemmed weapon shows "No Enhancement" + **Power Up** → `itemdefaultpattern` → a default **Common** gem.
- Applying a gem RECOMPUTES the secondary stats (`UpdatePattern.stats` = the new full `sta`). So: **gems → 6 primary stats (+ weapon Base/Wild, + flat HP) → secondary stats (ap/sp/tcr/scm/tha) → combat.**
